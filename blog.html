How to Calculate Percentages: A Simple Guide

Percentages are used everywhere—from shopping discounts and exam scores to taxes and financial calculations. Understanding the basic percentage formula makes many everyday calculations easier.

Formula Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example

Suppose you answered 45 questions correctly out of 60.

(45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%
