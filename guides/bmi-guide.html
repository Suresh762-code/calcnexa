Understanding BMI

BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight. It does not directly measure body fat or diagnose health conditions.

What it means

BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight. It does not directly measure body fat or diagnose health conditions.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)

Example

A person weighing 70 kg at 1.75 m has BMI ≈ 22.9.
