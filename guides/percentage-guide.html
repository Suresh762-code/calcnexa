How to Calculate Percentages

Percentages express a value as a fraction of 100. They are useful for discounts, scores, taxes, growth rates and comparisons.

What it means

Percentages express a value as a fraction of 100. They are useful for discounts, scores, taxes, growth rates and comparisons.

Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example

If 45 out of 60 answers are correct: (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%.
